ENGLISH
Grade 3
Three-Term Budget Of Work
For Learning Competencies
First Term
Content Standard
The learners demonstrate expanding vocabulary and understanding of high frequency words
and content-specific vocabulary; understand and create simple and compound sentences for
comprehending, analyzing, creating, and composing texts about regional themes and
content-specific topics.
Performance Standard
The learners use their expanding vocabulary of high-frequency and content-specific words, and
simple and compound sentences to comprehend, create, and compose narrative and
informational texts about regional themes and content-specific topics; and read grade level
texts with appropriate speed, accuracy, and expression.
